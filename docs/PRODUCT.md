# Product

Noryva — **Track less. Know more.**

Supporting proposition: **Nutrition and fitness without the friction.**

Founding rules:

- Earn registration before requesting it.
- Never make tracking harder to increase revenue.
- The diary works even when the servers don't.
- Calculated recommendations should be explainable.

Phase 1 is an entirely local mobile foundation: anonymous identity, goal/body/activity onboarding, calculated plan, home, local development food search, serving selection, and a persistent editable diary. It includes reset-local-data control.

Phase 1 excludes accounts and authentication, backend/API/database infrastructure, cloud sync, health/fitness providers, AI and media recognition, remote barcode data, monetisation, social features, ads, notifications, analytics/telemetry, web/admin products, and all named Phase 2+ product tiers.

Energy targets are estimates, not medical advice. Phase 1 uses Mifflin–St Jeor with the selected calculation sex, documented activity multipliers, goal adjustments, and deterministic 1,500 kcal male / 1,200 kcal female calculation floors. A result below its floor is clamped and the plan explicitly explains why.

Permanent product rule: **No advertising. No disruptive upselling.**
